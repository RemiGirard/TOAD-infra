TOAD / PROPOSED RECOVERY DRILLMarkdown source ↗

Disposable recovery drill awaiting authorization

No drill resources have been created. A read-only OpenStack lookup on 2026-10-27 confirmed that toad-drill-source and toad-drill-recovery do not already exist.

Exact proposed scope

  • Two separate operator contexts and Heat stacks: toad-drill-source and toad-drill-recovery, each using the maintained three-manager profile.
  • A synthetic application named proof, with only stack-owned volume proof_data. It contains generated demonstration records, no client data.
  • Dedicated drill DNS below drill.toad.remigirard.dev; any necessary NS delegation must be reviewed and authorized by exact record before creation.
  • Capture and verify an encrypted backup on the source stack. Restore only proof_data on the fresh recovery stack, then verify the known record count and checksum through the application.
  • Record cloud/image versions, hashes, replicas, required manual actions, actual data age, and elapsed recovery time. Run the ordinary verifier.
  • Delete only those two drill stacks after their exact deletion is authorized. Never restore to or destroy toad-prod.

Before running

Confirm the temporary cloud budget and quota, selected flavor, domains, isolated contexts, backup/identity escrow, and cleanup authorization. Two production-shaped stacks temporarily use six managers, two load balancers, and four reserved IPv4s. The cost worksheet is illustrative, not the drill's final quotation.

The exact destructive permissions needed are volume restore on the recovery stack and deletion of both named drill stacks. Parent-zone changes also require the exact record approval. This plan grants none of those permissions itself.

Until the drill is actually completed, the launch record continues to mark fresh-cluster service recovery as pending.
